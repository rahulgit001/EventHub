If you mean “is the whole project done?”: not completely. The booking, staff, inventory, catering, vehicle, payment, quotation, invoice, notification, reporting, and Docker workflows are implemented and locally verified. Remaining production work includes a real payment gateway, database migrations, and browser end-to-end tests.


Build a complete production-style full-stack web application called:

"EventHub – Event Rental & Booking Management System"

The application is inspired by a traditional Indian tent house/event rental business where customers book different services and rental items for weddings, birthdays, parties, religious events, corporate events and other functions.

The system should modernize the complete booking process.
